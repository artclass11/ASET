# ASET SignalDesk

SignalDesk is the product layer of ASET for turning structured company fundamentals into repeatable research output.

## Positioning
**Input:** a clean fundamentals CSV
**Processing:** validation + deterministic calculations
**Output:** compact research metrics and exportable reports

It is designed to be easy to demonstrate, easy to self-host, and easy to extend with licensed data providers.

## Free core
The repository includes a working local/browser demo and a Python engine. No account is required for the local workflow.

## Pro edition
The commercial edition can be licensed separately for:
- live provider integrations
- automated refresh and scheduled research jobs
- PDF and branded HTML reports

Commercial features are delivered as licensed software/services rather than simulated paywalls in the open-source repository.
